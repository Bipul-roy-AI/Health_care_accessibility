The study outputs

These outputs are not calculated from the current directory alone. The analysis pipeline requires explicit source approval and exports the underlying values alongside maps.

AnalysisMaps and measurementsRequired inputs
Physical accessibilityNearest-facility time; number of facilities reachable within chosen time thresholds; population with one or multiple options.Aligned population and travel-cost grids; selected DGHS tiers and travel assumptions.
Capacity coveragePopulation allocated service capacity and physically accessible population left unserved.DGHS service capacity in persons per declared period. Beds are not outpatient capacity.
ReferralModelled travel times, path distances and routes between specified facility tiers.Travel-cost grid and explicit origin/target type pairings.
Zonal coverageCovered population and percentage by division, district or union.Administrative polygons with stable IDs and population counts on the analysis grid.
New facilitiesAdditional people served by selected sites; candidate-location map and solver optimality status.Feasible candidate sites, capacity, site budget and the approved accessibility inputs.

New-site recommendations are conditional planning scenarios. They do not establish land availability, staffing, quality or approval to construct.
