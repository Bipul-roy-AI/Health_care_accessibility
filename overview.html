All matching listings, including inactive listings and offices.

Counts are institution listings, not population-adjusted access. Inactive listings and administrative offices are included; a listing does not confirm that a facility provides treatment.

Points are plotted only if they fall inside Bangladesh (OpenStreetMap outline, ODbL) and within 1 km of their recorded district, and do not share a coordinate with facilities in other areas. Division outlines: BBS/OCHA districts merged, via geoBoundaries (CC BY 3.0 IGO).
