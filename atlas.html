BANGLADESH / UPAZILA DISTANCE ATLAS

How far is the nearest hospital,
upazila by upazila?

A national 2 km grid measures the straight-line distance from every part of Bangladesh to the nearest mapped hospital in the DGHS registry. The results are summarised for 544 upazilas/thanas, 64 districts and 8 divisions, next to hospital points and approved beds.

Read this first: these are shares of land area, not of people, and straight-line kilometres, not travel times. Rivers, ferries and roads are ignored. Land-area figures overstate remoteness where land is uninhabited (for example the Sundarbans and char islands). Use the Hospital research page to measure from a specific place.

05 · Methods, limits and downloads

Hospitals

The hospital list is the site-wide HOSPITAL_TYPES definition used by the Statistics and Hospital research pages (25 DGHS facility types). Government means a hospital type with fully government, autonomous or semi-autonomous ownership. Everything else, including records with no reported ownership, is shown as non-government. Military and jail hospitals are part of the site definition, although public access to them is restricted. Only active records with published coordinates inside Bangladesh are used as points.

Distance surface

A 2 km grid covers the national boundary (— cells, UTM zone 46N). For each cell centre, the straight-line distance to the nearest point of each hospital class is computed with a k-d tree. Cells are assigned to districts and upazilas by polygon. Upazila figures with fewer than 5 cells are marked low-confidence, and 7 small Dhaka thanas contain no cell. The registry's coordinates are not verified on the ground, and the nearest listed hospital may not offer the service a person needs.

Boundaries

District and upazila polygons: Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS) and OCHA ROAP, redistributed by geoBoundaries (gbOpen BGD, simplified; CC BY 3.0 IGO). geoBoundaries tags the layer as boundary year 2020; its source dataset is titled "as of 2015". Upazilas were matched to the study's 544 BBS pcodes by district and name, all 544 one-to-one. These are ADM3 areas; union (ADM4) boundaries are not available.
