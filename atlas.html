Methods, limits and data

  • Facilities: active DGHS Facility Registry records. Hospitals = the 25 DGHS hospital types used on the Statistics page; government = government, autonomous or semi-autonomous ownership. Primary care = community clinics, union health centres, UH&FWC, MCWC and urban primary-care centres.
  • Location quality: a coordinate is used only if it lies within 1 km of the recorded district and is not shared with a facility recorded in another district or upazila. Flagged records stay in area totals.
  • Distance: 1 km grid in UTM 46N; straight-line distance from each cell centre to the nearest usable location. Areas with fewer than 5 cells are low-confidence; very small urban unions contain no cell.
  • Boundaries: BBS and OCHA ROAP via geoBoundaries (CC BY 3.0 IGO); the source dataset is "as of 2015". Divisions are dissolved from districts so all levels nest. Union IDs are not official BBS codes.
  • Not measured: population coverage, travel time, service quality or capacity. A registry listing does not confirm a facility is open or offers a service.
