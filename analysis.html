03 / INTERPRETATION

Know what each map measures

Listings by recorded area

Counts use DGHS administrative names, including facilities without coordinates. Joins require the parent area names. Unmatched names remain in the audit rather than being forced into an area.

Points inside polygons

Eligible published coordinates are assigned to the supplied boundaries. Missing locations are not moved to centroids. Shared-border ambiguity is excluded from point counts and reported.

Availability per resident

Division rates divide active listings by BBS 2022 residents and multiply by 100,000. District and upazila rates are unavailable because matching BBS population figures have not been supplied.

Zero means no matched listings or eligible points under the selected method. It does not establish an absence of care. Count/rate colour scales vary by tier; compare areas within a panel. Coordinate-completeness maps share a 0–100% scale, with grey where no name-joined listings exist.

What comes next?

Travel-time surfaces and population coverage need prepared transport inputs and finer BBS population data. Capacity coverage needs DGHS service capacity for a defined period; beds do not substitute for outpatient capacity. Referral routes and new-site scenarios will be added when the required inputs are validated.

04 / SOURCES & DATES

A saved, reproducible baseline

Source dates will appear when the analysis is loaded.

  • Health facilities: DGHS Facility Registry. Original IDs, area names and eligibility flags are retained.
  • Population: BBS Statistical Yearbook 2025, adjusted Census 2022, table 2.03, printed page 56 / PDF page 84. No population is spread across smaller areas.
  • Boundaries: user-supplied BBS/OCHA district and ADM3 layers. Geometry date: 2015; validOn: 13 November 2020. Divisions are derived from districts. Boundary metadata ↗

The baseline stays separately dated when the directory receives daily updates. Location-data gaps, historical boundaries and name differences can affect comparisons.
