Hospital inclusion rules and calculation definitions

Active means the DGHS status equals 1. DGHS-agency means the agency field equals DGHS; it does not imply government ownership. Government/autonomous uses the existing explicit ownership categories. Records with unknown agency are included only in appropriate broader scopes.

Coordinate completeness = eligible coordinates ÷ all selected active listings × 100. Eligible points must pass the published-coordinate flag and the project's pinned Bangladesh boundary check. Distinct registry IDs at identical coordinates are retained, because co-location does not establish duplication. Coordinates can still be imprecise.

District joins require an exact normalized name or a documented historical spelling alias and a matching parent division. Unmatched records are counted in selection totals and exposed in downloads, but are not assigned a district colour. A zero listing count is not proof of absence of care. An undefined completeness percentage is shown as missing, never zero.

Distances use the haversine formula on a sphere of mean radius 6,371.0088 km. Radius counts refer to eligible hospital listings, not people served. Ring counts use [0, 1] km for the first band and (lower, upper] thereafter. No population distribution is inferred from hospital density.
