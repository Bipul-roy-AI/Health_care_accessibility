05 / METHODS & SOURCES

Methods

Counting

Each DGHS ID is counted once per group; units on one campus can have separate IDs. Groups overlap, so do not add them. Administrative and training offices are not hospitals.

Dates

Registry status is not proof of a licence. The 2025 official totals and registry counts differ in date and definition; their gap is not growth.

Denominators

Rates use BBS adjusted Census 2022 division populations; no growth is assumed.

Division comes from the DGHS administrative label, not from map boundaries.

Hospital group and government-ownership rules

Government hospital = a hospital type below and an explicit government ownership value. Blank ownership is never assumed to be government.
