05 / METHODS & SOURCES

Know what you are comparing

Count records, not assumptions

Each DGHS institution ID is counted once within a selected group. Different IDs can represent units on the same campus. Groups overlap, so do not add their totals together. Administrative and training offices are excluded from the hospital group.

Keep dates visible

Registry status does not establish a valid current licence. The official 2025 totals and registry counts use different dates, coverage and definitions; their difference is not growth or a count of unlicensed facilities.

Use the right denominator

Rates use BBS’s adjusted Census 2022 division counts as republished in June 2026. No population growth is guessed. Rates are a baseline comparison, not a travel-time accessibility model.

Division comes from the DGHS administrative label. No map boundary or third-party population dataset enters these statistics.

Hospital group and government-ownership rules

Government-owned hospital listings require both a hospital type below and an explicit government ownership value. Facilities with missing ownership are excluded from this subset, even if their name suggests public ownership.
